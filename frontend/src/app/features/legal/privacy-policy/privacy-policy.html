3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ActivSwitzerland, we may collect the following categories of information.

When you create an ActivSwitzerland account, we may collect:

  • your name;
  • email address;
  • country;
  • encrypted password;
  • account creation date;
  • subscription status; and
  • other information you choose to provide.

We do not need to know your real-world identity to provide every feature of the Service, but some information is required to create and maintain an account.

3.2 Trip and User Information

If you use trip-planning or personalisation features, we may process information that you provide, including:

  • trip destinations;
  • planned routes;
  • travel dates;
  • preferences;
  • saved trips and itineraries;
  • notes or other User Content; and
  • information contained in conversations with the AI assistant.

Some of this information may indirectly reveal information about your interests or intended travel plans.

3.3 AI Assistant Conversations

If you use the ActivSwitzerland AI assistant, the information you enter into the conversation may be processed by third-party AI technology providers in order to generate responses.

Conversations may also be processed for purposes such as:

  • providing the AI service;
  • preventing abuse and misuse;
  • troubleshooting and debugging;
  • improving the Service; and
  • monitoring system performance and reliability.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into the AI assistant unless it is necessary for your use of the Service.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the AI assistant.

3.4 Payment Information

If you purchase an ActivSwitzerland subscription, payment information is processed by our payment provider, Stripe.

We may receive information from the payment provider such as:

  • email address;
  • payment status;
  • subscription type;
  • transaction information;
  • currency and amount paid; and
  • limited payment-method information.

Payment information is processed in accordance with the payment provider's own privacy policy and terms.

  • browser type and version;
  • operating system;
  • device type;
  • date and time of access;
  • pages or features accessed;

ActivSwitzerland may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies may be used for purposes including:

  • providing essential functionality;
  • understanding how the Service is used;
